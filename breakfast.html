WATCHDOG 🤖 Benefit-Watch — 2026-08-06

Watchdog report on German state benefits: what changed, when it bites, and who is affected. **5 tracked changes this run.** Every entry has an official or major-outlet source.

1. Wohngeld — Reform ("Neuordnung") im Kabinett: Leistungen sinken

Was hat sich geändert: Cabinet passed a draft law to "simplify and further develop" the Wohngeldgesetz. The Wohngeld formula coefficient **C is being adjusted downward**, which *reduces* payouts; Freibeträge change, notably for people with care needs (Pflegebedarf). Framed as budget stabilisation + bureaucracy reduction.

Ab wann: Draft stage (Referentenentwurf → Kabinett). Not yet in force — watch the Bundestag reading.

Wen betrifft's: All ~1.9m Wohngeld households, especially low-income renters and care-dependent claimants near the cut-off.

Tipp für dich: If you are close to the eligibility edge, **apply now** under current rules — entitlement is assessed at application date.

Quellen: https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/neuordnung-wohngeld-2445796 https://www.bmwsb.bund.de/SharedDocs/gesetzgebungsverfahren/DE/Downloads/referentenentwuerfe/wohngeldnovelle-2026.pdf https://www.sovd.de/aktuelles/meldung/stellungnahme-referentenentwurf-wohngeld-reform https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/gesellschaft/wohngeld-kuerzung-faq-100.html

2. Wohngeld — Haushalt 2026: Ansatz erhöht (+€150.13m)

Was hat sich geändert: Bundestag budget committee raised the 2026 Wohngeld budget line by **€150.13m** above the government draft (Einzelplan 25).

Ab wann: Budget year 2026.

Wen betrifft's: Signals capacity for continued payouts despite the cut-oriented reform — no direct change to individual entitlement.

3. Bürgergeld → "Neue Grundsicherung": harsher sanctions from 1 July 2026

Was hat sich geändert: Bürgergeld is renamed and restructured into the **neue Grundsicherung** (SGB II). Key points:

Ab wann: Phased from **1 July 2026** (law adopted March 2026).

Wen betrifft's: ~5.5 million recipients. Highest risk: people in high-rent cities (stricter KdU checks) and anyone who misses mail/appointments.

Tipp für dich: Always answer Jobcenter letters and cancel appointments in writing with a reason — the new rules punish no-shows fastest.

Quellen: https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/neue-grundsicherung-2399562 https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw10-de-grundsicherung-1150460 https://www.verdi.de/politik-gesellschaft/buergergeldgrundsicherung-2026-anspruch-voraussetzungen https://www.zeit.de/politik/deutschland/2026-03/buergergeld-grundsicherung-reform-koalition

4. Elterngeld — Prien draft: 14 → 12 months, 3 "Vätermonate", higher min/max

Ab wann: Not yet law; targeted for ~2027 births. Current births still under 14-month rules.

Wen betrifft's: ~1.61m Elterngeld recipients/yr (1.19m women, 417k men). Couples splitting leave unequally lose the most.

Quellen: https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/prien-elterngeld-102.html https://www.elterngeld.net/elterngeld-kuerzung.html https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/neuregelung-elterngeld-2268810

5. Kindergeld — €259/month since 01.01.2026, automatic payment planned

Was hat sich geändert: Kindergeld rose to **€259 per child/month** on **1 Jan 2026** (from €255). Coalition plans a two-stage rise toward **~€272**, plus **abolition of the application** (automatic payment via tax-ID linkage).

Ab wann: €259 in force now; automation and further increases still planned.

Wen betrifft's: All families with children — the automation step mainly helps families who currently never apply.

Quellen: https://www.smartunterhalt.de/ratgeber/gesetzesaenderungen/elterngeld-2026-ausblick-geplante-aenderungen https://www.sparkasse.de/aktuelles/reformen-eltern-2026.html
Two big cuts are in the pipeline (Wohngeld formula, Elterngeld duration) and one is already law (Grundsicherung sanctions from 1 July 2026). Only Kindergeld moved up. **If you might qualify for Wohngeld, apply before the reform passes.** *Generated by the Benefit-Discovery Watch agent. Draft laws can change — always verify with your local Wohngeldstelle / Jobcenter / Familienkasse.*
