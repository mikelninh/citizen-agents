**Opportunity:** DP31-202600059 / KI-Assistenzsystem Sozialleistungen

**Offer deadline:** 10 September 2026  
**Recommended route:** **SUBCONTRACT / PARTNER-FIRST**  
**Human approval required before outreach or commitments.**

## Why this matters

The public notice asks for a four-year framework covering an AI assistance system for social-benefit applications, including at minimum Wohngeld and Grundsicherung, plus operation, integration, API readiness, training, maintenance and support.

This overlaps strongly with our CARE / Public Service work, document-AI patterns, source/evidence layers and human-approval workflows. It does **not** mean we currently satisfy the full enterprise tender.

## Go / no-go

### Direct solo bid: NO unless all are proven

- required insurance coverage is available;
- required production references are satisfied;
- operational hosting/support capability is credible for the full term;
- all procurement documents and suitability criteria are met.

Public tender summaries indicate substantial reference and insurance requirements. Until the original documents prove otherwise, treat a solo bid as low-probability.

### Partner route: GO

Our aim is to become a small specialist work package inside a stronger prime contractor's offer.

## Our wedge

**Evidence-rich AI workflow for Wohngeld / Grundsicherung**

What we can credibly show now:

1. structured intake / document understanding patterns;
2. source-backed eligibility and rule reasoning prototypes;
3. provenance and evidence traces;
4. human approval for consequential actions;
5. replay / evaluation patterns for agent reliability;
6. API-first architecture prototypes.

What we must not claim:

- production deployment at Dataport scale;
- formal administrative decision authority;
- production references we do not have;
- guaranteed accuracy or savings.
