# Action Pack — BSI PRAKI 2026

**Opportunity:** Projekt 1000 — Agentic AI: Prüfanforderungen für LLM-basierte agentische künstliche Intelligenz (PRAKI)  
**Buyer:** Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (BSI)  
**Current public deadline:** 22 September 2026, 10:00  
**Recommended route:** **PARTNER-FIRST, with direct-bid review**  
**Human approval required before submission, outreach or commitments.**

## Why this is unusually strong for us

The procurement is explicitly about identifying important Agentic-AI use cases, deriving test requirements and generalising them for other use cases. That maps directly to work we already have around:

- tool compliance;
- completion integrity;
- human-approval boundaries;
- trace replay;
- loop detection;
- evidence and provenance;
- retrieval / citation verification;
- agent reliability evaluation.

The public notice also describes the project as suitable for SMEs. That improves plausibility, but it does **not** establish our formal suitability.

## Direct-bid gate

Do not choose direct bid until the full documents answer:

1. required company references;
2. required personnel CVs / qualifications;
3. turnover / insurance requirements;
4. consortium / subcontract rules;
5. deliverables and scoring model;
6. IP / publication requirements;
7. security requirements;
8. price structure.

If any major suitability requirement cannot be evidenced, switch to partner-first immediately.

## Partner-first wedge

**Agent Reliability Evaluation Work Package**

Offer a bounded contribution:

- threat/use-case taxonomy;

- adversarial and failure-mode cases;
- tool-use compliance checks;
- replayable traces;
- human-approval tests;
- evidence-backed benchmark report.

This is easier to defend than claiming we can deliver every workstream ourselves.
