## One-line venture

**Citizen Agents turns public changes, deadlines, funding opportunities and policy windows into source-backed Action Packs so organisations can act before the opportunity disappears.**

## Problem

Small and mid-sized organisations cannot continuously monitor every official source that can affect their funding, influence, compliance and mission. Existing monitoring creates feeds and alerts; the expensive part still remains: deciding what matters, proving why, and preparing the next action.

## Product

1. organisation enters its mission / website;
2. free Blind-Spot Scan finds a few source-backed historical signals;
3. the user judges whether they were known in time;
4. live protection monitors relevant official sources;
5. every strong signal becomes an Action Pack with evidence and next steps;
6. consequential external actions remain human-approved.

## Why now

- public information volume is growing;
- LLM agents make continuous monitoring and preparation technically feasible;
- organisations still need provenance, human approval and reliability boundaries;
- our own dogfood already surfaced time-sensitive tenders and grants relevant to our products.

## Business model

- Free — 3-signal Blind-Spot Scan
- €49 — Full Blind-Spot / Opportunity Report + 7 days protection
- €249/mo — Always-On Protection
- €499–999/mo — Team / portfolio intelligence
- €1,500+/mo — custom operational-awareness infrastructure / API / workflows

Pricing is a hypothesis until customers pay; no revenue should be claimed without evidence.

## Impact thesis

Better operational awareness should help organisations access funding earlier, participate in democratic windows in time, reduce avoidable deadline misses and spend less staff time manually scanning fragmented sources.

Impact is measured through observed user feedback and actions, not invented ROI.
