# Citizen Agents — Constraints

## Evidence
- every factual finding should point to a source;
- uncertain findings remain uncertain;
- source date/freshness and automation status should be inspectable.

## Claims
- no completeness claim until coverage is measured;
- working proof ≠ production readiness;
- programme budget ≠ user/customer value;
- estimated hours saved / guaranteed ROI remain unset until observed.

## Authority
- no autonomous legal or governmental decisions;
- agents draft/recommend; humans or consuming organisations approve consequential actions.

## Public-interest boundary
Core public-interest intelligence should remain inspectable and reusable even when commercial organisation workflows are added around it.
