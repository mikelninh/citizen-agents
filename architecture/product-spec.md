# Citizen Agents — Product Spec

## Core workflow
1. Watch approved public sources.
2. Detect/materialise a change with source evidence.
3. Normalise it into a structured civic signal.
4. Map who/what may be affected in the Impact Graph.
5. Ask: what can the user still do, by when, and why?
6. Present cited next actions with uncertainty/coverage state.
7. Capture usefulness feedback and observed outcomes.
8. Export proof/reliability reports without inventing ROI.

## Watchdog families
Rights & Money; Democracy Action Window; Funding Radar; Rights & Courts; Power & Influence; Elections/Petitions/Participation; Promises → Laws → Votes.

## Acceptance
A user should be able to trace a factual claim to its source and understand the most useful next step without confusing recommendation with authority.
