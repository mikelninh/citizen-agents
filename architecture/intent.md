# Citizen Agents — Intent

## Problem
Useful changes in rights, benefits, funding, laws and democratic participation are scattered across public sources. People and organisations often discover them too late, without context, or through untraceable summaries.

## Outcome
Turn public-source changes into **cited, inspectable signals and useful next actions** while keeping uncertainty, coverage gaps and human authority visible.

## Users
- citizens trying to understand what affects them and what they can still do;
- mission-driven organisations tracking funding, policy and action windows;
- teams consuming civic intelligence through APIs/feeds.

## North star
Observed usefulness: did the signal matter, was it already known, would it otherwise have been missed, and did it trigger a useful action?

## Non-goals
- claiming complete coverage before it is measured;
- autonomous legal/government decisions;
- turning programme budgets into invented customer ROI;
- hiding curated/manual work behind an automation label.
