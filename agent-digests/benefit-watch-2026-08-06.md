## 1. Wohngeld — Reform ("Neuordnung") im Kabinett: Leistungen sinken
- **What changed:** Cabinet passed a draft law to "simplify and further develop" the Wohngeldgesetz. The Wohngeld formula coefficient **C is being adjusted downward**, which *reduces* payouts; Freibeträge change, notably for people with care needs (Pflegebedarf). Framed as budget stabilisation + bureaucracy reduction.
- **Effective:** Draft stage (Referentenentwurf → Kabinett). Not yet in force — watch the Bundestag reading.
- **Who's affected:** All ~1.9m Wohngeld households, especially low-income renters and care-dependent claimants near the cut-off.
- **Citizen tip:** If you are close to the eligibility edge, **apply now** under current rules — entitlement is assessed at application date.

## 2. Wohngeld — Haushalt 2026: Ansatz erhöht (+€150.13m)
- **What changed:** Bundestag budget committee raised the 2026 Wohngeld budget line by **€150.13m** above the government draft (Einzelplan 25).
- **Effective:** Budget year 2026.
- **Who's affected:** Signals capacity for continued payouts despite the cut-oriented reform — no direct change to individual entitlement.
- **Source:** https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-1127152

## 3. Bürgergeld → "Neue Grundsicherung": harsher sanctions from 1 July 2026
- **What changed:** Bürgergeld is renamed and restructured into the **neue Grundsicherung** (SGB II). Key points:
  - Graduated 10/20/30 % sanctions are **abolished**; a breach can trigger a **direct 30 % Regelbedarf cut for 3 months**.
  - Missing a Jobcenter appointment without reason: from the **2nd missed appointment**, 30 % cut for 1 month.
  - **3 consecutive missed appointments → total loss of entitlement, including housing costs (KdU).**
  - **Vermittlungsvorrang** (placement-first) reintroduced; housing-cost "Angemessenheit" checked earlier and more strictly.
- **Effective:** Phased from **1 July 2026** (law adopted March 2026).
- **Who's affected:** ~5.5 million recipients. Highest risk: people in high-rent cities (stricter KdU checks) and anyone who misses mail/appointments.
- **Citizen tip:** Always answer Jobcenter letters and cancel appointments in writing with a reason — the new rules punish no-shows fastest.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/neue-grundsicherung-2399562
  - https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw10-de-grundsicherung-1150460
  - https://www.verdi.de/politik-gesellschaft/buergergeldgrundsicherung-2026-anspruch-voraussetzungen
  - https://www.zeit.de/politik/deutschland/2026-03/buergergeld-grundsicherung-reform-koalition

## 4. Elterngeld — Prien draft: 14 → 12 months, 3 "Vätermonate", higher min/max
- **What changed (draft, Ressortabstimmung as of 07.07.2026):**
  - Basiselterngeld shortened from **14 to 12 months**; partner months rise from 2 to **3**.
  - ElterngeldPlus max duration falls from **28 to 24 months**.
  - **Partnerschaftsbonus abolished** entirely.
  - First increase in ~20 years: minimum **€300 → €330**, maximum **€1,800 → €1,900**.
  - Income cap remains **€175,000** zu versteuerndes Jahreseinkommen (since 01.04.2025) — the earlier €200k/€150k thresholds are historic.
- **Effective:** Not yet law; targeted for ~2027 births. Current births still under 14-month rules.
- **Who's affected:** ~1.61m Elterngeld recipients/yr (1.19m women, 417k men). Couples splitting leave unequally lose the most.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/prien-elterngeld-102.html
  - https://www.elterngeld.net/elterngeld-kuerzung.html
  - https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/neuregelung-elterngeld-2268810

## 5. Kindergeld — €259/month since 01.01.2026, automatic payment planned
- **What changed:** Kindergeld rose to **€259 per child/month** on **1 Jan 2026** (from €255). Coalition plans a two-stage rise toward **~€272**, plus **abolition of the application** (automatic payment via tax-ID linkage).
- **Effective:** €259 in force now; automation and further increases still planned.
- **Who's affected:** All families with children — the automation step mainly helps families who currently never apply.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.smartunterhalt.de/ratgeber/gesetzesaenderungen/elterngeld-2026-ausblick-geplante-aenderungen
  - https://www.sparkasse.de/aktuelles/reformen-eltern-2026.html

### Bottom line for citizens
Two big cuts are in the pipeline (Wohngeld formula, Elterngeld duration) and one is already law (Grundsicherung sanctions from 1 July 2026). Only Kindergeld moved up. **If you might qualify for Wohngeld, apply before the reform passes.**
