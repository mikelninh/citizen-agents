## 1. Wohngeld — Kürzungsgesetz: konkretes Datum **1.1.2027**, drei Kürzungshebel
- **What changed (new detail since yesterday):** The cabinet adopted the Hubertz draft on **6 July 2026**; it is now in the Bundestag. Savings target: **€1.5bn in 2027, €2bn/yr from 2028**. Three concrete levers are now known:
  1. **Dynamisierung ausgesetzt** — no more automatic adjustment to inflation and rents (last adjustment 1.1.2025, next one would have been due 1.1.2027).
  2. **Heizkostenkomponente halbiert.**
  3. **Wohngeldformel geändert** — fewer households qualify; households at the upper income edge (today receiving roughly **€50–60/month**) lose the benefit entirely.
- **Effective:** planned **1 January 2027** (Bundestag debate pending).
- **Who's affected:** *All* current Wohngeld households (~1.2m in 2024 per Destatis; 44 % families, 52 % contain pensioners). Roughly **one third could lose entitlement completely** (Bauministerin Hubertz).
- **Existing Bescheide are not touched** — they run out normally, the cut bites at the next Weiterbewilligung.
- **Citizen tip:** Apply or renew **before 1.1.2027**. A Bescheid issued under 2026 rules keeps paying for its full 12-month period.

## 2. Wohngeld — **Neufestlegung der Mietenstufen** im Referentenentwurf
- **What changed:** The draft law re-sets the **Mietenstufen** (I–VII) nationwide. Per the ministry's own table, the distribution shifts: Stufe I falls from **27.9 % → 25.6 %** of municipalities, Stufe II **28.9 % → 26.9 %**, while the expensive Stufen IV–VII grow (IV 12.5 % → 13.5 %, V 6.0 % → 8.7 %, VI 3.6 % → 4.7 %, VII 2.0 % → 2.4 %).
- **Effective:** with the Novelle, planned 1.1.2027.
- **Who's affected:** Everyone — your **Mietstufe** directly sets the maximum rent counted in the Wohngeld formula. Households in municipalities moving *up* a Mietstufe partly offset the formula cut; those staying put absorb the full reduction.
- **Also in the draft (§ 26 Abs. 2):** alignment of Wohngeld with other priority benefits such as **Kindergeld and Kinderzuschlag**.
- **Source:** https://www.bmwsb.bund.de/SharedDocs/gesetzgebungsverfahren/DE/Downloads/referentenentwuerfe/wohngeldnovelle-2026.pdf

## 3. NEW ENTITLEMENT — **Rechtsanspruch auf Ganztagsbetreuung** in Kraft seit **1.8.2026**
- **What changed:** The legal right to all-day care for primary-school children (GaFöG) **came into force on 1 August 2026**, starting with **Klassenstufe 1** and rolling out year-group by year-group until all of grades 1–4 are covered (school year 2029/30).
- **Scope:** 8 hours/day on 5 weekdays including school time; closure periods max 4 weeks/year (GaFöG). The KMK's minimum definition for Ganztagsschulen is *below* the GaFöG entitlement — worth knowing if your school offers less.
- **Who's affected:** All families with a child entering class 1 in school year 2026/27. This is an **enforceable individual right against the local youth-welfare/school authority**, not a discretionary offer.
- **Citizen tip:** If no place is offered, put your demand **in writing** to the Jugendamt/Schulträger — the claim is legally enforceable.
