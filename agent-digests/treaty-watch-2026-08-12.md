## 2. ECHR (Council of Europe) — Moldova: Court finds a fair-trial violation (Babii, 2 Jul 2026)
- **Treaty:** European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR), Article 6 §1 — right to a fair trial.
- **Country:** Republic of Moldova.
- **What happened:** In *Babii v. the Republic of Moldova* (judgment of 2 July 2026, Merits and Just Satisfaction), the Court found a **violation of Article 6 §1**. The case concerned the retroactive restarting of a civil limitation period and the resulting breach of legal certainty.
- **Why it matters to ordinary people:** When courts or legislatures rewrite time-limits *after the fact*, ordinary people can suddenly lose their day in court. This ruling protects the basic predictability of justice for everyone dealing with civil claims.

## 3. UN — Universal Periodic Review (UPR): 56th session to scrutinise Greece, Hungary, Thailand, Ireland & others
- **Treaty / mechanism:** UN Human Rights Council — Universal Periodic Review (all UN members reviewed every 4.5 years on their human-rights obligations).
- **Countries:** The upcoming 56th session is scheduled to review, among others, **Greece, Hungary, Thailand, Ireland, Tanzania, Tajikistan, Papua New Guinea, Eswatini, Trinidad and Tobago, Suriname, Samoa, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Antigua and Barbuda**.
- **What happened:** The OHCHR UPR homepage lists the States due for their peer-review this cycle. Each undergoes a public interactive dialogue and receives recommendations it is expected to implement.
- **Why it matters to ordinary people:** Your government must answer to its peers — and to you — on its rights record, and the recommendations are public. Citizens can use them to hold their own state accountable between elections.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.ohchr.org/en/hr-bodies/upr/upr-home
