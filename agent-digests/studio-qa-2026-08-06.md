## Static review — the things I specifically went looking for

Deleting a module and rewriting the bot AI in one commit is exactly where regressions hide. I checked and cleared:

- **Dangling imports of the deleted `weapon.js`** — none anywhere in `src/`, `server/`, `index.html`. Clean removal.
- **Scratch-vector aliasing.** `_v`/`_v2` are module-level shared `THREE.Vector3`s (botbrain.js:18-19) reused across four functions. `botShoot` passes `_v` as the *read* target and `_v2.clone()` as the *write* out-param, so `leadTarget` never writes into the vector it is reading. This is correct, but it is one careless edit away from not being.
- **Double damage.** The old path had botbrain call `c.onHit` directly. The new path spawns a projectile that reports through `_onProjectileHit`. I confirmed botbrain no longer calls `onHit` at all, so a bot hit is reported exactly once, by the host that owns the bot.
- **`this.bots` exists** before `_botShoot` reaches for it — initialised in `mode.js:18`.
- **Timer leak in room.js** — `setInterval` at :46, `clearInterval` at :231. Unchanged, still balanced.

## Issues

**MEDIUM — bots don't pay for their shots.** This is the one worth fixing before this feels right. A player's fire is gated by `charge.ready` with per-tool `cost`/`regen` (match-mode.js:1016-1023). A bot is gated only by `tool.rate`. On the Splat Blaster (`rate: 0.1`) a bot can sustain ~10 shots/s at 9 soak — about 90 soak/s, forever — while a human on the identical tool is throttled to roughly 6/s by `cost: 0.055` against `regen: 0.34`. The bot out-sustains the player by ~1.7x on the exact weapon it's holding. The projectiles are dodgeable now, which is the whole point of the change, but an infinite-ammo stream is a different kind of unfair. *Fix:* give each bot a `Charge` from `weapons/tools.js`, or stopgap with `fireCooldown *= Math.max(1, tool.cost / tool.regen)`.

**LOW — dead `onHit` surface.** `ctx.onHit` is still passed at match-mode.js:761 and still documented in the tacticalBrain JSDoc, but nothing calls it now. Leaving a wired-up damage callback lying around is how the double-damage bug gets reinvented in three weeks. Delete it.

**LOW — `bot._track` never pruned.** Keyed by target id, entries for dead/disconnected players persist for the bot's lifetime. Bounded at 8 players so it is not a practical leak — just clear it on round reset.

**LOW — bots are ranged-only.** `BOT_TOOLS` deliberately omits `leafblade`. That is a reasonable scope call for one session, but it means solo and 2v2 practice never teaches a player to read an incoming saber. The director's BOTH directive is only half-served by the bots until a melee archetype exists. Flagging as next session's work, not as a defect in this PR.

## What I could NOT test — read this part

Automated QA cannot playtest feel, and this PR is *entirely* about feel. Specifically untested:

- **Whether led shots read as fair.** The claim is "it missed for a reason you can see on screen." I can verify the maths is sound; I cannot verify it looks sound at 60fps.
- **Whether projectiles are actually dodgeable** by a moving human.
- **Multiplayer host-authority for bot hits.** No second client was connected. The ownership logic was reviewed statically only, never exercised over the wire.
- **Whether bots visibly hold the tool they now fire** — no rendered-model check is possible headless.
- **`aimError` difficulty tuning** (`(1 - accuracy) * 3.2`). No data on real hit rates against a strafing human. This number is a guess until someone plays it.
- **Explore mode** builds and shares no changed files, but was not visually confirmed in a browser.
