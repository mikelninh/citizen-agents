# 🌐 Truth-Watch — 2026-08-07

Watchdog digest on disinformation narratives **and their debunks**. No claim is listed without a credible fact-check attached.

---

## 1. "Video shows migrant rush into Ceuta" — FALSE
**The claim:** A viral TikTok/X video allegedly shows masses of migrants storming the Spanish exclave of Ceuta.
**What's true:** The footage shows football fans celebrating/rioting after a PSG win; a boy's words in the clip were mistranslated and instrumentalised.
**Debunk:** AFP Fact Check (04/08/2026) — https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.C3VT6KE · CORRECTIV (04/08/2026) — https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/08/04/video-zeigt-feiernde-fussballfans-keine-menschen-in-ceuta/
**Why it matters:** Recycled sports footage relabelled as migration is one of the cheapest, fastest-spreading formats in EU migration disinfo.

## 2. Fake *Tagesschau* item on Berlin CSD attack — FORGED
**The claim:** A screenshot of an ARD *Tagesschau* news item claims the Federal Prosecutor found Russian involvement in the attack on Berlin's Christopher Street Day.
**What's true:** The *Tagesschau* item is a fabrication — it was never published.
**Debunk:** CORRECTIV (29/07/2026) — https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/07/29/csd-anschlag-tagesschau-meldung-zu-angeblicher-russischer-beteiligung-ist-gefaelscht/
**Why it matters:** Forged public-broadcaster branding is a recurring attack on trust in German media; always check the original tagesschau.de item.

## 3. "Spain is building 5,000 new mosques" — UNSUPPORTED
**The claim:** A TikTok account claims Spain plans 5,000 new mosques due to a growing Muslim population.
**What's true:** No evidence exists for any such plan; no official source supports the figure.
**Debunk:** CORRECTIV (06/08/2026) — https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/08/06/5-000-neue-moscheen-fuer-spanien-tiktok-account-streut-unbelegte-geruechte/

## 4. "Spokane wildfires were set to clear land for data centres" — NO EVIDENCE
**The claim:** Viral posts allege the Spokane (US) wildfires were deliberately started to make room for data centres.
**What's true:** No evidence supports arson-for-data-centres; investigators have made no such finding.
**Debunk:** AFP Fact Check (05/08/2026) — https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.C3VR39E
**Pattern:** A climate/tech conspiracy template that reappears after nearly every major wildfire.

## 5. AI-altered audio of Indonesian minister — MANIPULATED
**The claim:** Clip of an Indonesian minister's remarks about government-backed cooperatives.
**What's true:** The audio was altered using AI; the minister did not say the quoted words.
**Debunk:** AFP Fact Check (07/08/2026) — https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.C43V4WE

## Platform & policy response (EU Digital Services Act)

Enforcement accelerated sharply over July 2026:
- **10 July** — Commission preliminarily found Instagram/Facebook "addictive design" in breach of the DSA. https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1579
- **16 July** — Commission accepted X's action plan on its ad repository and researcher data access, following the December 2025 non-compliance decision and fine. https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-accepts-xs-action-plan-comply-digital-services-act
- **20 July** — AliExpress fined €550m for failing to mitigate risks from illegal products. https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1654
- **24 July** — TikTok preliminarily found in breach over minors' account safety. https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminary-finds-tiktok-breach-digital-services-act-failing-ensure-safe-accounts-minors

Researcher data access (item 2 above) matters directly for disinformation work: X's accepted plan is the first concrete remedy on Article 40 access.
Overview: https://techpolicy.press/europes-summer-of-digital-services-act-enforcement-targets-platform-design
