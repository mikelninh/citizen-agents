## Fund 1 — Anti-SLAPP-Richtlinie (EU) 2024/1069
**Richtlinie:** Anti-SLAPP-Richtlinie (EU) 2024/1069. **Frist:** 7.5.2026 (verfehlt). **Ministerium:** BMJ. **Status:** VERSÄUMT — Vertragsverletzungsverfahren eröffnet 15.7.2026 gegen 14 MS. **Bürger:innen-Auswirkung:** Schutz vor Einschüchterungsklagen für Whistleblower, Journalist:innen und zivilgesellschaftliche Akteur:innen fehlt in Deutschland. **Quelle:** EUR-Lex + EAPIL (siehe Quellenliste).

### EN
- **Headline:** Germany misses the 7 May 2026 deadline to transpose the EU Anti-SLAPP Directive (EU) 2024/1069.
- **What changed:** The transposition deadline passed; on 15 July 2026 the Commission opened infringement proceedings against 14 Member States, including Germany, for failing to notify complete transposition.
- **Why:** Germany's draft transposition act was still "close to completion" but not notified, so EU anti-SLAPP protections are not yet in force in German courts.
- **Effective:** Not yet in force in Germany.
- **Deadline:** 7 May 2026 (missed).
- **Who's affected:** Journalists, NGOs, activists and citizens who speak out on matters such as the environment or corruption.
- **How:** No uniform early-dismissal and cost-protection rules against abusive "SLAPP" suits exist in German courts yet.
- **Citizen tip:** If you face an abusive defamation/silencing suit for public-interest speech, document it and note that EU minimum protection should already apply in spirit; seek legal aid specialised in media/SLAPP defence.

## Fund 2 — Entgeltgleichheits-Richtlinie / Pay Transparency (EU) 2023/970
**Richtlinie:** Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970. **Frist:** 7.6.2026 (verfehlt). **Ministerium:** BMAS. **Status:** VERSÄUMT — kein Gesetz verabschiedet; nur Bundestags-Mitteilung 16.7.2026. **Bürger:innen-Auswirkung:** Arbeitnehmer:innen können Auskunfts-, Berichts- und Gehaltsbandbreiten-Rechte nicht einheitlich durchsetzen; Wirksamkeit der Berichtspflichten erst Juni 2028. **Quelle:** EUR-Lex + Ius Laboris / DLA Piper (siehe Quellenliste).

### EN
- **Headline:** Germany misses the 7 June 2026 deadline to transpose the EU Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970.
- **What changed:** No transposition act adopted; the Bundestag published only a short notice on 16 July 2026. Entry into force is expected no earlier than early 2027; pay-reporting duties are not effective until June 2028.
- **Why:** The competent ministry plans to amend the existing Pay Transparency Act rather than create a new framework, and a draft bill has not yet been published.
- **Effective:** Early 2027 at the earliest; reporting duties June 2028.
- **Deadline:** 7 June 2026 (missed).
- **Who's affected:** Workers in Germany seeking equal-pay enforcement, salary-range disclosure and gender-pay-gap reporting.
- **How:** Existing German law (Entgelttransparenzgesetz) does not uniformly implement the Directive's new rights.
- **Citizen tip:** Ask your employer for pay-range information in job ads and use existing German pay-transparency rights; document refusals, as the full EU rights are delayed by years.
