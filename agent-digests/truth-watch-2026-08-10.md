## 1. "Fifa hat Argentinien von der WM 2030 ausgeschlossen"
- **The false narrative:** Posts claim FIFA president Infantino officially excluded Argentina from the 2030 World Cup in July 2026.
- **What's false:** No such decision exists. FIFA made no announcement; the claim traces back to fabricated/satirical content recirculated as news.
- **Debunk:** CORRECTIV.Faktencheck, 07.08.2026 — https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/08/07/nein-die-fifa-hat-argentinien-im-juli-nicht-offiziell-von-der-wm-2030-ausgeschlossen/
- **Why it matters:** Sports rumours are a cheap, high-reach vehicle for training audiences to trust fabricated "official announcement" formats.

## 2. "Spain is building 5,000 new mosques"
- **The false narrative:** A TikTok account claims Spain plans to build 5,000 mosques because of a growing Muslim population.
- **What's false:** There is no evidence for any such plan — no government programme, no source document. The number is an unsubstantiated rumour.
- **Debunk:** CORRECTIV.Faktencheck, 06.08.2026 — https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/08/06/5-000-neue-moscheen-fuer-spanien-tiktok-account-streut-unbelegte-geruechte/
- **Pattern:** Anti-migrant "replacement" framing built on invented statistics.

## 3. Ceuta migration video — mistranslated and miscontextualised
- **The false narrative:** A video circulating on TikTok is presented as showing migrants at the Spanish exclave of Ceuta, with a boy's words translated as a threat.
- **What's false:** The footage shows Moroccan football fans celebrating a World Cup win on 4 July (e.g. in the Netherlands); the boy's words are mistranslated.
- **Debunk:** CORRECTIV.Faktencheck, 04.08.2026 — https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/08/04/video-zeigt-feiernde-fussballfans-keine-menschen-in-ceuta/
- **Related:** AFP Fact Check also found fake images of a "Moroccan couple leaving their wedding to rush to the Ceuta border" — https://factcheck.afp.com/

## 4. Fake "Tagesschau" report on the Berlin CSD attack
- **The false narrative:** A screenshot styled as an ARD Tagesschau news item claims Russian involvement in the attack on Berlin's Christopher Street Day.
- **What's false:** The Tagesschau item is forged. No such report was published and no such finding was announced.
- **Debunk:** CORRECTIV.Faktencheck, 29.07.2026 — https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/07/29/csd-anschlag-tagesschau-meldung-zu-angeblicher-russischer-beteiligung-ist-gefaelscht/
- **Why it matters:** Forged public-broadcaster branding is one of the most damaging disinformation formats in Germany — it borrows institutional trust directly.

## 5. AI-generated disaster and crisis footage keeps spreading
- **The false narrative:** Multiple clips/images circulated in early August as real events: a SpaceX moon collision, a damaged Japanese shopping centre after the July 2026 quake, HAARP "causing" the Japan quake.
- **What's false:** AFP verified these as AI-generated, recycled, or fabricated; HAARP cannot induce earthquakes.
- **Debunks (AFP Fact Check):**
  - Fake SpaceX moon collision video — https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.C46B6WF
  - AI image of damaged Japanese shopping centre — https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.C3MY463
  - HAARP earthquake conspiracy revival — https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.C3YL7NR

## Platform & policy response — EU Digital Services Act

July 2026 was the most active enforcement month in the DSA's life so far, and it sets the frame for how these narratives get handled:

- **10 July 2026** — Commission preliminary findings: Instagram and Facebook's *addictive design* breaches the DSA. https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1579

- **Second systemic-risk report** on very large platforms and search engines published, naming "prominent and recurrent" risks including information integrity. https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/second-report-systemic-risks-very-large-online-platforms-and-search-engines-under-digital-services

**Citizen takeaway:** researcher data access under the DSA is the single most important lever for detecting these campaigns early. X's compliance plan being accepted matters more for public accountability than any individual takedown.
