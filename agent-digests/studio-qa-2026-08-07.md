## 🔴 HIGH — the dodge's i-frames don't exist in real PvP

This is the finding of the day.

Hit resolution in this game is **shooter-authoritative**. `_onProjectileHit` (match-mode.js:1061) says so plainly:

```js
if (item.owner !== this.localId) {
  // Someone else's shot: we only draw it, the owner reports it.
```

Only the shooter sends `{type:'hit', target, damage}`. And `server/room.js` has no `hit` or `damage` logic at all — it's a relay (`config`/`start`/`input`/`ev`/`chat`/`ping`).

Now look at what the dodge actually does (match-mode.js:625):

```js
const hittable = this.ctx.player.iframes > 0
  ? parts.filter((p) => p.id !== this.localId)
  : parts;
```

That removes **the local player** from **the local client's** target set. But a remote human shooting you resolves that hit in *their* client, which has never heard of your `player.iframes`. Their shot lands.

So the i-frames work against bots and your own local simulation — and do nothing at all against the eight friends the game is built for. Worse, the HUD pip flashes its blue `.active` glow while this happens, so the player is told they dodged and then takes the damage anyway. A defensive mechanic that lies to you is worse than no defensive mechanic.

**Fix options, cheapest first:**
1. Broadcast the dodge — an `ev` message or an `inv` flag on the existing state tick — so remote clients filter a dodging player out of their own `parts` before resolving. Small change, fits the current relay architecture.
2. Victim veto — the target's client rejects an inbound `hit` that fell inside its i-frame window, and damage applies only on ack. More correct, more work.
3. Or scope it honestly: drop the i-frames and the glow, ship the roll as a pure mobility burst. Still a great addition.

Option 1 is what I'd do this week.

## 🟠 MEDIUM — the roll is secretly an air-dash

`dodge()` gates on `canMove`, `frozen`, `crouching`, `swimming` — but never on `this.grounded`. Double-tap in mid-air and you get a 14 m/s horizontal burst that hard-overrides `vel.x/vel.z` (player.js:260-265), bypassing the deliberately low air accel (14) and air friction (0.6).

Jump-then-dodge crosses noticeably more ground than the movement tuning intends. On a tiny hand-made planet that's a map-skip risk. The code comment calls the roll "a decision, not a movement modifier" — the air case makes it a traversal tool. Add `if (!this.grounded) return false;`, or keep the air-dash deliberately at a lower speed with no i-frames. Right now it's an accident rather than a choice.

## 🟠 MEDIUM — the dodge only counters one weapon family

The `hittable` filter is applied to `projectiles.update()` and `_driveBots()`. But the local melee/soak path at match-mode.js:988 fires its own `{type:'hit', damage: stats.soak}` and never consults `iframes`.

So the roll dodges projectiles and not melee. The lead's **BOTH** decision says sabers and projectiles are both first-class — a defensive verb that only answers one of them breaks the rock-paper-scissors before it's built. Route all damage through one `_tryHit(target, damage)` helper that checks invulnerability once.

## 🟡 LOW

- **Water edge case** (player.js:220): entering water zeroes `dodgeTime` but leaves `iframes` and `dodgeCooldown` running — up to 0.12s of free invulnerability while swimming with the burst already cancelled, and the pip briefly shows "active" with no roll happening. Zero `iframes` in the same guard.
- **Bot targeting coupling** (match-mode.js:630): the filtered list also goes to `_driveBots()`, so for 0.12s the player vanishes from the bots' world entirely, not just from hit resolution — bots may drop and re-pick targets rather than simply missing. Pass full `parts` for targeting, filter only at resolution.
- **Bundle size**: 927 kB single chunk, pre-existing, growing every feature. `manualChunks` for three.js. Director's call, not mine.

## What I could NOT test — read this part

Automated QA cannot playtest. Specifically:

- **Feel.** Whether 0.35s duration / 14 m/s / 0.12s i-frames reads as crisp or floaty is a human judgement. Nobody has held the keyboard.
- **Real multiplayer.** The HIGH issue above is derived from *reading* the hit-resolution path, not from watching a desync happen live. `puppeteer-core` has no bundled Chrome on this runner, so `tools/mptest.mjs`, `mpdiag.mjs`, `iframe-test.mjs` and `shot.mjs` all failed to launch. Setting a `channel` or `executablePath` would unlock real E2E for every future run — worth doing.
- **Ergonomics of the 0.28s window.** The unit test proves the state machine, not whether normal strafe-tapping in a firefight triggers false-positive dodges. My instinct says 0.28s is on the generous side. Needs hands.
- Rendering of the `.dodge-pip` HUD and the roll animation. Mobile/touch. Performance at 8 players.

## Bottom line

The dodge roll is good work — clean, tested, well-commented, correct math. It builds, the server boots, nothing regressed. But its signature feature, the i-frames, is invisible to every other human in the match. Fix the replication (or drop the i-frames and ship the burst), and this is a clear SHIP.
