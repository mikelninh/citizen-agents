# 🌡️ Climate-Watch Digest — 2026-08-12

*Citizens deserve to know who's actually cutting emissions and who's greenwashing.
Here's today's gap between climate promises and reality. Every claim links to a
primary or credible source.*

## 1. Global emissions hit a new record — the Paris path is losing
- **Pledge:** Under the 2015 Paris Agreement, ~200 countries committed to reach
  net-zero CO₂ by 2050 to limit warming to 1.5 °C.
- **Reality:** The IEA's *Global Energy Review 2026* finds energy-related CO₂
  emissions rose **~145 million tonnes in 2025 to a new high of nearly 38.4
  billion tonnes (Gt)** — about **5% above 2019 levels**.
- **Who's affected:** Everyone; record emissions push warming further past the
  1.5 °C guardrail.
- **Source:** https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2026/co2-emissions

## 2. National climate pledges fall far short of the math
- **Pledge:** The 2025 round of NDCs is supposed to put the world on a 1.5 °C
  path by 2035.
- **Reality:** UNFCCC's *2025 NDC Synthesis Report* (64 new NDCs, ~30% of global
  emissions) projects only a **17% cut (range 11–24%) below 2019 levels by
  2035**. UNEP's *Emissions Gap Report 2025* says **35–55% cuts are needed by
  2035** for 2 °C / 1.5 °C. The pledge is roughly **2–3× too weak**.
- **Who's affected:** Citizens in frontline and vulnerable nations who pay the
  price of insufficient action.
- **Sources:**
  - https://unfccc.int/process-and-meetings/the-paris-agreement/nationally-determined-contributions-ndcs/2025-ndc-synthesis-report
  - https://www.unep.org/resources/emissions-gap-report-2025

## 3. The US turns its back on the IEA's net-zero mandate
- **Pledge:** The US signed the Paris Agreement, pledging net-zero by 2050.
- **Reality:** In Feb 2026, US Energy Secretary Chris Wright gave the IEA an
  ultimatum — drop net-zero scenarios or lose the US as a member (the US pays
  ~$6M/yr of the IEA's $22M budget). The administration is also rolling back
  power-plant air rules and unfettering fossil-fuel development.
- **Who's affected:** Global climate-governance credibility; US communities near
  coal plants.
- **Source:** https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/sustainable-switch-climate-focus-us-issues-ultimatum-iea-over-net-zero-goals-2026-02-21/

## 4. Corporate "net-zero" pledges riddled with greenwashing red flags
- **Pledge:** Hundreds of firms advertise net-zero or carbon-neutral commitments.
- **Reality:** A 2026 *Nature* study ("Red flags in green promises") found
  **11% of net-zero pledges fail to cover all emission scopes** and **10% have a
  lobbying record contradicting their climate commitments**. Carbon Market Watch
  concludes nearly all current corporate climate claims are misleading,
  exaggerated or false.
- **Who's affected:** Consumers and investors misled into funding laggards.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s44168-026-00346-6
  - https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2023/03/09/zeroing-in-on-greenwashing-how-corporations-misuse-net-zero-pledges/

## 5. EU and UK ratchet up long-term targets — watch the follow-through
- **Pledge:** EU countries finalized a 2040 target to cut emissions **90%** vs
  1990; the UK set a **~87%** cut by 2040 (announced June 2026).
- **Reality to watch:** The EU is simultaneously planning slower ETS CO₂ cuts and
  more free permits for industry — a tension between headline ambition and
  near-term delivery.
- **Who's affected:** EU/UK citizens; sets the benchmark for other governments.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/eu-countries-give-final-approval-2040-climate-target-90-emissions-cut-2026-03-05/
  - https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/britain-sets-87-emissions-reduction-target-2038-2042-period-2026-06-02/
  - https://www.reuters.com/business/environment/eu-make-carbon-market-more-flexible-industrial-companies-2026-07-08/
