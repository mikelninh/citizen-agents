## 1. Global promise: "emissions peak and fall". Reality: another record year.

**Pledge:** Paris Agreement pathways require global CO2 to fall ~45% from 2010 levels by 2030 (1.5 °C, no/limited overshoot).

**Reality:** IEA's *Global Energy Review 2026* reports energy-related CO2 rose again in 2025 — up ~0.4% (+145 Mt) to a new record of nearly **38.4 Gt**, which is **5% above 2019 levels**. Atmospheric CO2 hit ~**427 ppm**, +2.4 ppm on 2024 and ~50% above pre-industrial.

**Who's affected:** everyone. The remaining 1.5 °C carbon budget is described by the Global Carbon Project as "virtually exhausted".

**Gap:** promised −45% by 2030; actual trajectory is still upward, 14 years after Paris.

- https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2026/co2-emissions
- https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/

## 2. The one genuinely good number: clean tech is already cutting 3 Gt/year.

**Reality check in the other direction:** IEA quantifies avoided emissions from deployed clean technology in 2025:

| Technology | CO2 avoided per year |
|---|---|
| Solar PV | 1.5 Gt (≈ half of India's annual CO2; half of it in China) |
| Wind | 1.1 Gt (≈ France + Germany + Italy combined) |
| Nuclear | 210 Mt |
| Electric cars | 100 Mt |
| Heat pumps | 90 Mt |

**Why it matters:** emissions growth slowed to the weakest rate since 2021 *because* of this. Deployment works; it is simply not yet outrunning demand growth.

- https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2026/co2-emissions

## 3. Government targets: NDCs still point to 2.3–2.5 °C, policies to 2.8 °C.

**Pledge:** hold warming to "well below 2 °C", pursuing 1.5 °C.

**Reality:** UNEP's *Emissions Gap Report 2025* finds full implementation of all NDCs yields **2.3–2.5 °C** this century; **current policies** yield **2.8 °C**. UNFCCC's own NDC synthesis work shows 2030 emissions under submitted NDCs landing roughly **15.9% above the 2010 level**, versus the −45% needed.

**Who's affected:** citizens in every country whose government signed Paris — and disproportionately those in low-lying and drought-exposed states who did not cause the emissions.

- https://www.unep.org/resources/emissions-gap-report-2025
- https://unfccc.int/process-and-meetings/the-paris-agreement/nationally-determined-contributions-ndcs/ndc-synthesis-report/ndc-synthesis-report

## 4. Corporate net-zero: 96% of assessed companies show a greenwashing red flag.

**Pledge:** thousands of listed companies advertise "net zero" or "carbon neutral".

**Reality:** a peer-reviewed analysis in *npj Climate Action* (2026) using CDP, InfluenceMap and Net Zero Tracker data finds **96% — 3,455 of the companies assessed — exhibit at least one indicator of net-zero or climate-mitigation greenwashing**: long time horizons, heavy offset reliance, incomplete (Scope 3) accounting. Net Zero Tracker covers 1,977 listed companies plus 198 countries. Earlier NewClimate/Carbon Market Watch monitoring found headline "net-zero" targets of major corporates actually imply only **~40%** aggregate emission cuts, not 100%.

**Who's affected:** consumers and investors paying a green premium for claims that do not survive audit.

- https://www.nature.com/articles/s44168-026-00346-6
- https://zerotracker.net/
- https://newclimate.org/resources/publications/corporate-climate-responsibility-monitor-2022

## 5. Enforcement is arriving: EU green-claims rules bite from 27 Sept 2026.

**What changes:** the Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition (ECGT) Directive bans vague, unsubstantiated environmental claims; **EU Member States must enforce from 27 September 2026**. The Commission issued guidance in December 2025. Precedent already set: in **November 2025, 21 airlines committed** to changing their environmental marketing after Commission/CPC enforcement action.

**Citizen takeaway:** from late September, "climate neutral flight/product" labels without verifiable substantiation are illegal in the EU — and reportable to your national consumer authority.
