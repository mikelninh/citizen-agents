## 1. Global emissions hit a new record — while pledges promise cuts

**The pledge:** Paris Agreement, net-zero by mid-century; almost every government says emissions must fall now.

**The reality:** Energy-related CO₂ emissions rose by ~145 Mt in 2025 to a new all-time high of nearly **38.4 Gt CO₂** (IEA, Global Energy Review 2026). The Global Carbon Project independently puts fossil+cement CO₂ up **1.1% in 2025 to a record 38.1 Gt**.

**Who's affected:** everyone. Record emissions = record atmospheric CO₂ concentration, locking in more warming.

## 2. New national climate plans (NDCs) still fall far short of 1.5°C

**The pledge:** Governments submitted new NDCs for the 2035 target year, framed as "raised ambition".

**The reality:** UNFCCC's update finds global GHG emissions would fall only **~12% by 2035 vs. 2019** under the new NDCs. Science-aligned 1.5°C pathways require roughly **60% below 2019 by 2035**. UNEP's Emissions Gap Report 2025 projects **2.3–2.5°C** warming with full NDC implementation, and **2.8°C** on current policies.

**Who's affected:** every citizen in a country whose government calls its NDC "1.5°C-aligned" — the math says otherwise.

- https://unfccc.int/news/update-to-ndc-synthesis-report-shows-the-emissions-curve-is-being-bent-downwards-but-urgent
- https://unfccc.int/process-and-meetings/the-paris-agreement/nationally-determined-contributions-ndcs/ndc-synthesis-report/ndc-synthesis-report
- https://www.unep.org/resources/emissions-gap-report-2025

## 3. 96% of corporate net-zero claims show at least one greenwashing red flag

**The pledge:** Thousands of companies advertise "net zero" and "climate neutral".

**The reality:** A 2026 peer-reviewed analysis in *npj Climate Action* (Nature portfolio) applied a red-flag framework to corporate claims and found **96% — 3,455 of the companies analysed — display at least one indicator of net-zero or mitigation greenwashing** (long horizons, heavy offset reliance, incomplete scope accounting). Earlier NewClimate/Carbon Market Watch monitoring found self-declared "climate leaders" commit to cutting their actual footprint by only **~36–40%** by their claimed net-zero date, not 100%.

**Who's affected:** consumers and investors paying a premium for "green" products and funds.

- https://www.nature.com/articles/s44168-026-00346-6
- https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2023/03/09/zeroing-in-on-greenwashing-how-corporations-misuse-net-zero-pledges/
- https://newclimate.org/resources/publications/corporate-climate-responsibility-monitor-2022

## 4. Enforcement is finally arriving — offset-based "climate neutral" claims get banned

**The pledge:** Airlines and consumer brands sold "carbon neutral flying" and "climate neutral" labels.

**The reality:** After coordinated EU/CPC-Network action, **21 airlines committed (announced 5 Nov 2025) to modify their environmental marketing claims**. From **September 2026** the EU's Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition directive (ECGT) bans generic green claims and offset-based product "climate neutral" claims EU-wide.

**Who's affected:** EU consumers — the "climate neutral" sticker on products is on a legal countdown.

- https://www.bdlaw.com/publications/eu-enforcement-action-21-airlines-commit-to-reform-environmental-claims-what-aviation-and-non-aviation-businesses-should-know/
